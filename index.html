A Grammar Escape Room — 5 Investigators Required

The Mission

The tomb of Pharaoh Neferkara has been sealed for 3,000 years. A team of investigators has broken through the outer wall — but four cursed chambers stand between you and the burial chamber. Each chamber holds a piece of the Pharaoh's true name. Only by speaking it correctly can the curse be lifted. Each investigator holds secrets the others don't. You must work together.
